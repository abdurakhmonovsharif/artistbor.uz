Privacy Policy

Artistbor Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Artistbor collects, uses, stores, shares, and protects personal information when you use the Artistbor mobile application, website, and related services.

Effective date September 15, 2026
Service region Republic of Uzbekistan
Privacy contact privacy@artistbor.uz
Support https://artistbor.uz/help

1. Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to the Artistbor Android and iOS applications, Artistbor web services, artist and client accounts, artist profiles, bookings and orders, uploaded media and documents, payment-confirmation features, notifications, and customer-support functionality.

Artistbor is intended for use in Uzbekistan and is available in Uzbek, Russian, and English.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Account and authentication information

When you register or use Artistbor, we may collect:

  • first name and last name;
  • phone number;
  • email address, if provided;
  • profile photo;
  • account role or type;
  • preferred language;
  • account and authentication identifiers; and
  • other information you voluntarily add to your account.

Artistbor currently uses phone-number registration with a one-time verification code (OTP). During demo or testing stages, OTP verification may be handled directly by Artistbor. If a third-party SMS/OTP provider is introduced, this Privacy Policy will be updated where appropriate.

2.2 Artist profile information

Artist profiles may contain:

  • name;
  • avatar or profile image;
  • biography;
  • services and service categories;
  • prices;
  • portfolio images or files;
  • rating and reviews;
  • region or service area; and
  • other professional information voluntarily provided by the artist.

2.3 Booking and order information

For bookings and orders, Artistbor may process:

  • selected artist and service;
  • booking date and time;
  • order status;
  • service price;
  • booking location or address;
  • payment status;
  • uploaded payment confirmation;
  • order history;
  • cancellation or rescheduling information; and
  • other information necessary to fulfil the order.

2.4 Uploaded images and files

Users may upload images and files, including profile images, portfolio content, documents, booking-related files, or payment confirmation images. Uploaded content is transmitted through the Artistbor backend and stored on Artistbor-controlled server infrastructure in Uzbekistan.

2.5 Technical information

We may process technical information such as:

  • device and operating-system information;
  • application version;
  • IP address;
  • push-notification token;
  • login and security events;
  • server and application logs; and
  • basic diagnostic information necessary to operate and secure the service.
Artistbor currently does not use advertising SDKs, personalised advertising, Firebase Analytics, or Firebase Crashlytics.

3. Device Permissions

Artistbor may request the following permissions:

Location

Location is requested only when a location-dependent feature is used, such as selecting or determining a service location or finding relevant nearby services. Artistbor does not require background location access for normal operation.

Camera

Camera access may be used when you choose to capture an image for a profile, portfolio, booking, document, or payment confirmation.

Photos, gallery, and files

Artistbor may access selected photos or files when you choose to upload them.

Microphone

Artistbor does not currently request microphone access.

Notifications

Notification permission is used to send order updates, reminders, service messages, new concert or event information, and relevant discount or promotional notifications.

You may manage permissions at any time through your device settings. Some features may not function without the relevant permission.

4. How We Use Information

Artistbor may use personal information to:

  • create and manage accounts;
  • authenticate users;
  • display artist profiles and portfolios;
  • provide service search and booking functionality;
  • manage orders and booking status;
  • verify payment confirmations;
  • show relevant contact information after the required booking conditions are met;
  • send operational and promotional push notifications;
  • provide customer support;
  • prevent abuse, fraud, or fake payment confirmations;
  • maintain platform security;
  • resolve disputes; and
  • comply with applicable legal requirements.

5. Visibility of Information Between Users

Artist information visible to clients

A client may see an artist's public profile information, including: name, avatar, biography, services, prices, portfolio, rating, reviews, region, and artist contact information made available through the platform.

The artist's phone number may be visible to the client so that the client can contact the artist regarding a service.

Client information visible to artists

A client's phone number becomes visible to the selected artist after the booking has been confirmed and the required advance payment has been completed or verified.

Artistbor does not currently provide an in-app client-to-artist chat feature.

6. Payment and Payment Confirmation

Artistbor currently does not process card payments through an integrated card-payment gateway.

Where advance payment is required, Artistbor may display payment instructions or a card/account number. The client makes the transfer using an external banking application or payment service and may then upload a receipt, screenshot, or other payment confirmation to Artistbor.

Payment-confirmation information may include:

  • payment amount;
  • payment date and time;
  • transaction/reference information;
  • payer or recipient information visible on the receipt;
  • masked account or card information visible on the receipt;
  • uploaded receipt image or file; and
  • the related order or booking identifier.
Artistbor does not ask users to provide CVV/CVC, PIN codes, online-banking passwords, or other secret banking credentials.

A payment confirmation may be accessible to the client who uploaded it, the relevant artist, and authorised Artistbor administrators or operators where access is required for verification, order handling, support, or dispute resolution.

7. How We Share Information

Artistbor does not sell personal information.

Information may be shared:

  • between clients and artists where necessary to provide the booked service;
  • with authorised Artistbor administrators and operators;
  • with infrastructure or notification service providers where technically necessary;
  • where required by applicable law or a lawful request; or
  • with the user's consent.

Artistbor's backend and uploaded media/files are hosted on server infrastructure located in Uzbekistan.

8. Firebase Cloud Messaging and Push Notifications

Artistbor uses Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM), a Google service, to deliver push notifications.

For notification delivery, technical data may include:

  • push-notification token;
  • application instance information;
  • device or operating-system information; and
  • information necessary to route and deliver the notification.

Notifications may include:

  • booking and order reminders;
  • booking status changes;
  • payment-related updates;
  • service notifications;
  • new concert or event information;
  • discounts or promotional announcements; and
  • important account or security messages.

Users may disable push notifications through device settings.

9. OpenStreetMap and Location

Artistbor uses map functionality based on OpenStreetMap to support location-related features.

Location information is accessed only when the user requests or enables a feature that needs location. Artistbor does not use location for cross-app advertising tracking.

10. Data Retention

Artistbor retains personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy.

  • Active account data: retained while the account is active.
  • Soft-deleted accounts: retained in a disabled state for up to 90 days.
  • Backups: generally retained on a rolling basis for up to 30 days.
  • Payment confirmations: retained for as long as reasonably necessary to verify the related payment, manage the order, handle disputes, prevent fraud, and meet applicable requirements.
  • Security and operational logs: retained for a reasonable period necessary for platform security and diagnostics.

11. Account Deletion

Users can request account deletion from within the Artistbor app:

Profile → Settings → Delete account

Users can also review the account-deletion instructions at: https://artistbor-uz.vercel.app/privacy-term#account-deletion

Deletion process: after a user requests deletion, the account is soft-deleted and access is disabled. The account may remain in soft-deleted status for up to 90 days. After that period, personal information associated with the account is permanently deleted or anonymised, except where limited retention is necessary for fraud prevention, unresolved disputes, security, or applicable legal obligations.

Backup copies may remain temporarily for up to 30 additional days until the relevant backup cycle is overwritten.

A suspended, blocked, or temporarily disabled account is not the same as a permanent account-deletion request.

12. Your Rights and Choices

Depending on applicable law, you may request to:

  • access information associated with your account;
  • correct or update account information;
  • delete your account and personal information;
  • withdraw device permissions;
  • disable push notifications;
  • request information about how your data is processed; and
  • contact Artistbor regarding a privacy concern.

Privacy requests may be sent to privacy@artistbor.uz.

13. Children's Privacy

Artistbor is intended for users aged 13 and older. Users aged 13 or older may register for an Artistbor account, subject to applicable law.

Artistbor does not knowingly allow children under 13 to create an account. If we become aware that personal information from a child under 13 has been collected improperly, we will take reasonable steps to delete it.

14. Data Security

Artistbor uses reasonable technical and organisational safeguards designed to protect personal information against unauthorised access, disclosure, alteration, misuse, or loss.

These measures may include encrypted HTTPS communication, authentication controls, restricted administrative access, server access controls, logging, and other security measures appropriate to the platform.

No internet transmission or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

15. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when Artistbor features, data practices, service providers, legal requirements, or app-store requirements change.

The latest version will be published on this page with an updated effective or last-updated date.

Номер телефона клиента становится доступен выбранному артисту только после подтверждения заказа и внесения или подтверждения необходимой предоплаты.

В Artistbor в настоящее время нет встроенного чата клиент–артист.

6. Оплата и подтверждение оплаты

Artistbor в настоящее время не принимает банковские карты через интегрированный платежный шлюз.

При необходимости Artistbor показывает номер карты или счета. Клиент самостоятельно переводит средства через банковское приложение или платежный сервис и затем загружает чек, скриншот или другое подтверждение оплаты.

Artistbor не запрашивает CVV/CVC, PIN-код, пароль от интернет-банкинга или иные секретные банковские данные.

Подтверждение оплаты может быть доступно самому клиенту, выбранному артисту, а также авторизованным администраторам и операторам Artistbor для проверки, поддержки и разрешения споров.

7. Передача данных

Artistbor не продает персональные данные.

  • между клиентом и артистом, когда это необходимо для оказания услуги;
  • авторизованным администраторам и операторам;
  • техническим сервис-провайдерам, когда это необходимо для работы сервиса;
  • если этого требует закон;
  • с согласия пользователя.

8. Firebase Cloud Messaging и push-уведомления

Artistbor использует Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) от Google для доставки push-уведомлений.

  • push-токен;
  • данные экземпляра приложения;
  • данные устройства/операционной системы;
  • иная техническая информация, необходимая для доставки уведомления.

9. OpenStreetMap и геолокация

Artistbor использует картографическую функциональность на базе OpenStreetMap.

Геолокация используется только когда пользователь включает функцию, которой она требуется, и не используется для рекламного трекинга.

10. Сроки хранения

  • Активный аккаунт: пока аккаунт активен.
  • Soft-deleted аккаунт: до 90 дней.
  • Резервные копии: до 30 дней по циклу резервного копирования.
  • Подтверждения оплаты: столько, сколько разумно необходимо для проверки платежа, обработки заказа, разрешения споров и предотвращения мошенничества.
  • Системные логи: разумный срок, необходимый для безопасности и диагностики.

11. Удаление аккаунта

В приложении:

Profile → Settings → Delete account

Веб-страница: https://artistbor-uz.vercel.app/privacy-term#account-deletion

После запроса на удаление аккаунт переводится в soft-delete и доступ к нему блокируется. Он может храниться в таком состоянии до 90 дней. После этого персональные данные удаляются окончательно или анонимизируются, за исключением ограниченных данных, которые необходимо сохранить для безопасности, предотвращения мошенничества, незавершенных споров или выполнения применимых требований.

Данные в резервных копиях могут временно сохраняться еще до 30 дней, пока соответствующий backup не будет перезаписан.
